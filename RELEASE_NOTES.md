# Release notes — v1.0.0 (versionCode 1)

First public release.

**Check a message.** Paste any SMS, WhatsApp message or link and get a clear
verdict with plain reasons: Safe, Be Careful or Dangerous. All of it runs on your
phone, with no network.

**Check a screenshot.** Pick a screenshot or take a photo; the text is read on
the device and checked by the same engine.

**Check a payment QR.** Scan a UPI QR before you pay. It warns about collect
requests, pre-filled amounts and look-alike payee names.

**Check a number.** Type a number and see whether its format looks unusual.

**Voice.** Speak your message, and hear the answer read aloud.

**If you were scammed.** The golden hour, 1930, freezing your card and UPI,
reporting on cybercrime.gov.in, and saving evidence as a PDF.

**Learn.** A game to spot scams, this week's scams, a call triage wizard, a
family code word, and a helper wizard for setting up an elder's phone.

**Saathi Agent (optional, off by default).** Message watch, call screening, a
daily phone-health check, and a remote-access warning. Every switch is off until
you turn it on, and everything runs on this phone.

**Call Guard (started only by you).** Live help during a call, with a visible
notice while it listens. It never records audio and keeps no transcript.

**Languages.** Hindi and English are complete. Thirty more Indian languages are
listed and switchable, marked beta, and fall back to Hindi for anything not yet
translated.

**Privacy.** No account, no ads, no analytics, nothing uploaded. Delete all data
in one tap.

This is a helpful check, not a guarantee. No app can catch every scam.
